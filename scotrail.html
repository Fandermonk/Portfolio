Edinburgh Festival
× ScotRail

Encouraging festival travel through an integrated campaign concept.

Project overview

As part of a degree project supporting the 2021 Edinburgh International Festival, I developed a campaign concept with ScotRail that encouraged visitors to make the train part of their festival journey. The work covered social media strategy, content creation, Google Display advertising, website copywriting, SEO content, styling, photography, railway advertising and bus-station advertising.

The opportunity

Make the journey part of the experience.

The Edinburgh International Festival brings world-leading artists and audiences together across dance, opera, music and theatre. The campaign positioned rail travel not as a functional necessity, but as part of the cultural experience itself: "Your festival experience starts on the journey."

Festival awarenessTravel considerationScotRail journeyEdinburgh Festival

Social media content

Festival excitement meets travel inspiration.

Social media content used documentary-style photography, ScotRail's established blue identity and bold festival yellow typography. Three concept directions were developed: Festival Journey (the journey starts here), Experience Edinburgh (discover something extraordinary), and Festival Travel (your next adventure starts on the train).

Railway advertising

Native to the railway journey.

The campaign was extended into railway environments — platform billboards, station concourses and illuminated advertising panels — designed to feel native to the travel experience. Large-format photography, high-contrast messaging and strong yellow typography ensure readability at distance.

Bus station advertising

Extending visibility beyond rail.

The campaign was adapted for public advertising environments outside railway stations — bus shelters, bus stations, transport hubs and high-footfall city locations. This extended campaign visibility to reach people while they were already considering travel.

Google Display advertising

A consistent system across every digital format.

A range of Google Display creatives translated the campaign into multiple advertising dimensions — leaderboard, mobile banner, medium rectangle, large rectangle, skyscraper and wide billboard. Each format maintains the consistent visual identity: festival imagery, ScotRail branding, yellow accents and clear calls to action.

The campaign system

One idea across every touchpoint.

The campaign demonstrated how a single concept — making the journey part of the experience — can be adapted across social, display, website, railway, bus station and festival environments while maintaining a consistent visual and strategic identity. Four campaign concepts were developed, with two selected for further project development.
