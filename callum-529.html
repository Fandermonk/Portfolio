CALLUM 529

A different kind of whisky — where automotive design meets Scottish whisky-making.

Project overview

CALLUM 529 by Annandale is a limited-edition, unpeated single malt Scotch whisky, presented in a bespoke ceramic bottle. The campaign connected automotive design culture with Scottish heritage across paid media, a landing page and a two-part onboarding journey.

The idea

Position the bottle as a designed object.

The campaign moved beyond conventional whisky advertising. Deep navy, ceramic surfaces, warm amber and editorial typography framed CALLUM 529 as a meeting of precision, craftsmanship and attention to detail.

DesignCraftHeritageExperience

Social launch

Build familiarity through product detail and lifestyle.

Social content carried the campaign's visual world into a feed-led format: bottle photography, sculptural detail, tasting moments and the CALLUM × Annandale collaboration. Each post helped introduce a different part of the product story.

Conversion

Give the product story a premium destination.

The landing page brings together the release story, product specification, collaboration and the bespoke ceramic bottle. It lets campaign traffic move from a first impression into deeper product discovery.

LinkedIn + Meta → Product story → Landing page → Product exploration

Onboarding

Continue the story in the inbox.

The two-email sequence makes the introduction feel editorial rather than promotional. Email 01 welcomes people to "a different kind of whisky"; Email 02 explores the shared pursuit behind CALLUM × Annandale.

The system

One premium world across every touchpoint.

LinkedIn and Meta created awareness; social and photography expanded the design-and-craft narrative; the landing page supported deeper discovery; and the onboarding sequence maintained the relationship after sign-up.
