Furnich AR

Life beyond the manual — an augmented reality mobile app replacing confusing paper diagrams with interactive, real-world assembly guidance.

Project overview

Furnich AR is an augmented reality mobile concept designed to make flat-pack furniture assembly easier, more intuitive, and frustration-free. Instead of deciphering paper manuals, searching through video tutorials, or hiring costly handymen, users receive step-by-step, real-time spatial guidance directly in their room.

01 / The problem

Flat-pack furniture is accessible. Assembling it is not.

Ready-to-assemble furniture gives customers flexibility, but the assembly experience is fraught with friction. Users traditionally face three flawed options:

01 / Paper manualsVague diagrams, unlabelled screws, and confusing sequences lead to errors and dismantled parts.
02 / Online videosRepeatedly pausing, rewinding, and wiping greasy fingers across a phone screen slows the process down.
03 / Hiring handymenIntroduces additional costs, scheduling delays, and removes the satisfaction of building it yourself.
“Make furniture assembly feel like following a visual guide in the real world rather than interpreting an abstract manual.”
64%of 1,301 surveyed consumers prefer building flat-pack furniture themselves, yet struggle with unclear manuals and missing tools. The opportunity wasn't eliminating DIY — it was removing the frustration.

A frictionless path from box to built.

03 / Design iteration

From feature-heavy directory to focused tool.

Early wireframes and concepts explored e-commerce style browsing with complex multi-level filters, directory listings, and heavy menus. Testing revealed that users assembling furniture needed fast clarity, not an e-commerce catalog.

Iterating toward simplicity: the homepage was stripped down to prioritize recent scans, scannable furniture categories, and immediate camera/QR access.

04 / Product discovery

Calm, scannable, and clutter-free.

The refined home screen provides clean visual previews of furniture, streamlined category filters (Chair, Sofa, Bed, Desks), and a persistent navigation bar with quick access to the AR camera.

05 / Preparation & tools

Prepare first. Assemble second.

One of the most frustrating moments in DIY assembly is discovering missing tools halfway through the build. Furnich AR makes required tools explicit on the product page before the user begins.

Layered support model

To ensure users never get stuck, Furnich AR integrates a three-tier support structure:

01 / AR GuidanceReal-time 3D spatial alignment showing where parts lock in.
02 / Visual DocsClean component breakdowns and hardware counts.
03 / AI AssistantContextual troubleshooting chatbot answering tool and assembly questions on demand.

06 / Spatial AR guidance

In-context guidance directly in the room.

The AR camera view serves two crucial functions:

Before assembly: Users can preview the furniture in their physical space to check dimensions, fit, and aesthetic compatibility.

During assembly: Computer vision and AR overlays highlight exactly where screws belong, which panel connects next, and which orientation is correct.

07 / Instant QR access

Zero-friction entry from physical packaging.

Instead of manually searching for model names or complex serial codes, users scan the QR code printed on the flat-pack box or store receipt. The app immediately identifies the furniture and loads the interactive 3D guide.

08 / Complete ecosystem

Connected flow across physical and digital touchpoints.

Furnich AR connects discovery, physical packaging, tool preparation, and hands-free assembly into one cohesive design language.

Touch

Fast, ergonomic mobile navigation for browsing, filtering, and bookmarking products.

Augmented Reality

Spatial 3D instructions anchored to the physical parts in real time.

Voice Control

Hands-free navigation ("Next step", "Repeat step") while holding tools and panels.

Gesture Navigation

Contactless gestures allowing users to step through instructions without touching the phone.

09 / Principles & takeaways

Designing for the physical environment.

Reduce cognitive load

Show only what the user needs to do in the immediate step rather than an overwhelming 40-page diagram booklet.

Make information visual

Hardware, screws, and tools are rendered with clear visual icons and 3D overlays.

Keep critical actions accessible

QR scanning and camera modes are always accessible in one tap from anywhere in the app.

Innovation grounded in utility

AR technology was used strictly to solve real assembly friction, never as a gimmick.

Furnich AR was developed as an 8-month UX concept and interaction design study (Jan–Aug 2021). The case study presents the verified design decisions, information architecture, and interface deliverables without fabricated metrics.
