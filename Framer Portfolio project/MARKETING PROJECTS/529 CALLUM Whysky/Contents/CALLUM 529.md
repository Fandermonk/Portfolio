# CALLUM 529 × Annandale Distillery

## A digital launch campaign for a limited-edition Scotch whisky collaboration

**Project type:** Brand campaign, web direction and CRM  
**Client collaboration:** CALLUM Designs × Annandale Distillery  
**Scope:** Landing-page design, email marketing, social creative and campaign direction

CALLUM 529 brought together British automotive design and Scotch whisky craftsmanship in a limited-edition release. The digital campaign needed to communicate the character of the product — its sculptural ceramic decanter, technical design language and distillery heritage — while giving collectors a clear, premium route to learn more and enquire.

## The challenge

The product lives at the intersection of luxury, engineering and heritage. The campaign needed to preserve that sense of rarity across every touchpoint without becoming overly technical or losing the emotional story behind the bottle.

## My role

I contributed to the digital campaign direction across:

- Luxury landing-page UI and conversion structure
- Editorial campaign copy and visual storytelling
- VIP email and CRM concepts
- Social-media curation and content direction
- Alignment between digital assets and physical launch collateral

## Creative direction

The visual system draws on the bottle’s glossy royal blue finish, ribbed sculptural form and racing-inspired orange accent. Industrial patina, copper-toned surfaces and restrained typography connect the automotive influence to Annandale’s Scottish distilling heritage.

## The digital experience

### A product-led landing page

The web direction uses an immersive hero, large product imagery and a paced storytelling flow. It introduces the collaboration, explores the design and origin of the whisky, presents the product specification and makes an enquiry or reservation path visible throughout.

### A VIP email sequence

The email concepts were designed to carry the same premium narrative beyond the site. The sequence covers the reveal, craft and origin story, and collector-focused release communication, each with a focused call to action.

### A considered social system

The social direction combines product details, design studies, heritage imagery and lifestyle moments. This gives the campaign a coherent visual rhythm while allowing each post to highlight a different part of the story.

## Design principles

**Let the product lead.** The decanter is the central campaign asset, so photography and large visual scale do most of the work.

**Use restraint to signal luxury.** Dark surfaces, generous spacing and a limited colour palette create a refined rather than crowded experience.

**Connect narrative and conversion.** Enquiry calls to action appear naturally after moments of product discovery instead of interrupting the editorial flow.
