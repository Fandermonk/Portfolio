# CALLUM 529 by Annandale — Integrated Marketing Campaign Case Study

## Project Overview

**Brand:** CALLUM  
**Product:** CALLUM 529 by Annandale  
**Category:** Premium Scotch Whisky  
**Campaign Focus:** Brand Awareness, Product Storytelling, Premium Positioning, Digital Acquisition & Customer Engagement

CALLUM 529 by Annandale is a limited-edition, unpeated single malt Scotch whisky, distilled by Annandale and selected and presented by CALLUM in a bespoke ceramic bottle.

The project combined **automotive design culture with Scottish whisky heritage**, requiring a premium marketing system that could communicate craftsmanship, exclusivity and design without making the product feel like a conventional whisky campaign.

The campaign materials developed for the project included:

- LinkedIn advertising
- Webinar LinkedIn carousel
- Meta advertising
- Conversion-focused landing page
- Onboarding email sequence
- Premium email/newsletter creative
- Product storytelling content
- Campaign messaging and CTAs

# 02 — The Marketing Challenge

CALLUM 529 sits at the intersection of two distinct worlds:

**Automotive design**

and

**Scottish whisky craftsmanship**

The campaign therefore needed to avoid looking like a standard whisky advertisement.

Instead, the creative direction positioned the product as a **designed object and premium experience**, using restrained layouts, cinematic photography, deep navy tones, ceramic textures, whisky amber and editorial storytelling.

### Communication Challenge

The audience needed to understand:

- What CALLUM 529 is
- Where the whisky comes from
- Why CALLUM is involved
- What makes the bottle distinctive
- Why the product is limited
- How automotive design and whisky craftsmanship connect

---

# 03 — Campaign Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- Establish CALLUM 529 as a premium design-led whisky
- Introduce the collaboration between CALLUM and Annandale
- Build awareness around the limited-edition release
- Communicate the craftsmanship behind the product
- Drive traffic to the product experience
- Create a consistent premium identity across channels
- Build an onboarding journey for interested audiences

### Brand Positioning

The campaign was built around the idea that **exceptional design and exceptional whisky share the same values: precision, craftsmanship and attention to detail.**

# 20 — Strategic Value of the Project

CALLUM 529 was an opportunity to market a product where **design itself is part of the value proposition**.

Instead of communicating only:

**“This is a whisky.”**

the campaign communicated:

**“This is a designed object, a Scottish whisky and a meeting of two craftsmanship-led worlds.”**

That distinction shaped the creative direction across every touchpoint.

---

# 21 — Project Takeaway

The CALLUM 529 project demonstrates how a premium product can be marketed through a connected ecosystem rather than isolated advertisements.

The campaign connected:

**Brand Story**  
→ CALLUM × Annandale

**Product**  
→ Limited-edition single malt

**Design**  
→ Bespoke ceramic bottle

**Experience**  
→ Whisky, tasting and lifestyle

**Acquisition**  
→ LinkedIn + Meta

**Conversion**  
→ Landing page

**Engagement**  
→ Two-step onboarding sequence

The result was a consistent marketing narrative across paid media, web and CRM communication.
