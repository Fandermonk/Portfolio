# Edinburgh International Festival × ScotRail

## Encouraging festival travel through an integrated campaign concept

**Project type:** Degree collaboration project  
**Client context:** Edinburgh International Festival in partnership with ScotRail  
**Focus:** Campaign strategy, social content, styling, photography direction, website copy and SEO content

The Edinburgh International Festival brings world-leading artists and audiences together. As part of a degree project supporting the 2021 festival, I worked on a campaign concept with ScotRail that encouraged visitors to make the train part of their festival journey.

## The opportunity

The festival attracts a broad mix of audiences: attendees, artists, organisers and visitors arriving from different locations. The campaign needed to make travel feel like an enjoyable part of the cultural experience while communicating the practical benefits of arriving by rail.

## My contribution

I helped develop an eight-part strategy that informed four campaign concepts, two of which were selected for further development. My work included:

- Social-media content and campaign direction
- Social-media strategy
- Styling and photography support
- Website copywriting and SEO content
- Audience-focused messaging

## Campaign idea

The creative direction positions the train as a gateway to the festival: a convenient, lower-friction way to arrive ready to explore Edinburgh. Bold yellow type, documentary-style festival imagery and ScotRail’s established blue identity create a high-energy visual system that works across posters, station placements and digital channels.

## What the work demonstrates

### A connected travel story

The campaign uses the same promise across physical and digital moments: travel to Edinburgh, discover the city and experience the festival. This consistency helps a visitor understand the message wherever they encounter it.

### Design for real-world contexts

Out-of-home concepts were created for railway environments, where messaging must be readable quickly and still carry the excitement of the festival.

### Content that supports the decision

The content plan considered the information people need before travelling: why to choose rail, what the festival offers and how the journey can feel part of the occasion.

## Reflection

The project strengthened my ability to develop campaign work beyond one format. It required a clear central idea, practical audience thinking and a visual direction flexible enough to work across social, web and physical advertising.
