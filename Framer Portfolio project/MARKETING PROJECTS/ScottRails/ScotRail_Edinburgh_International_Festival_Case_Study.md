# ScotRail × Edinburgh International Festival — Integrated Marketing Campaign Case Study

## Project Overview

**Brand / Partner:** ScotRail × Edinburgh International Festival  
**Project:** Edinburgh International Festival 2021 travel and promotional campaign  
**Role:** Marketing / Digital Marketing Project  
**Focus:** Social Media, Campaign Strategy, Content, SEO, Copywriting, Digital Advertising, Outdoor Advertising and Travel Promotion

I worked on a campaign connected to the **Edinburgh International Festival 2021**, developed in partnership with **ScotRail**, with the aim of encouraging festival audiences to travel to Edinburgh by rail and making the journey part of the overall festival experience.

The project formed part of a degree project and exposed me to a broad range of marketing activities, including social media strategy, content development, photography/style direction, website copywriting, SEO content and campaign advertising.

The 2021 Edinburgh International Festival returned to live performance from **7–29 August 2021**, after the disruption caused by COVID-19. The official Festival described the 2021 edition as a return to live and outdoor performance, with COVID safety a priority. citeturn0search14turn0search9

# 01 — The Campaign Context

The Edinburgh International Festival brings together world-leading artists and audiences across dance, opera, music and theatre.

For the 2021 edition, the Festival returned to live performance in Scotland with a programme running from **7–29 August 2021**. citeturn0search14

The campaign opportunity was to connect:

**Festival Experience + Travel Experience**

Rather than treating transport as a functional necessity, the campaign positioned rail travel as part of the journey to Edinburgh.

ScotRail has historically supported travel to Edinburgh's festivals through festival-focused services, promotional activity and travel information. Its festival communications have included enhanced services, additional capacity and late-night travel options. citeturn0search10turn0search3

---

# 02 — Project Challenge

The campaign needed to communicate the excitement of Edinburgh's festival season while also making rail travel feel:

- Convenient
- Relevant
- Accessible
- Enjoyable
- Connected to the festival experience

The challenge was particularly relevant in the context of the 2021 return to live events, when audience confidence, travel information and clear communication were important considerations.

The campaign therefore had two connected communication goals:

### Festival Goal

Create excitement around the Edinburgh International Festival.

### Travel Goal

Encourage audiences to consider ScotRail as part of their journey to Edinburgh.

---

# 03 — Campaign Objectives

### Primary Objectives

- Promote the Edinburgh International Festival
- Increase awareness of festival travel opportunities
- Encourage travel by rail
- Communicate the convenience of travelling to Edinburgh by train
- Connect ScotRail with the festival experience
- Create social and digital content around the campaign
- Extend the campaign into physical railway and public advertising environments
- Develop a consistent campaign identity across multiple channels

---

# 04 — Target Audience

The campaign was designed around people travelling to Edinburgh for the festival.

### Audience Groups

- Festival attendees
- Visitors travelling from other parts of Scotland
- Local Scottish audiences
- Cultural tourists
- People planning festival trips
- Families and groups travelling together
- Existing rail users
- Potential festival visitors considering transport options

### Audience Insight

Festival travel is not only about reaching the destination.

It is about:

**Planning → Travelling → Arriving → Experiencing the Festival**

The campaign aimed to make ScotRail relevant across this journey.

---

# 05 — Strategic Idea

## MAKE THE JOURNEY PART OF THE EXPERIENCE

The core strategy was to connect the excitement of the Edinburgh International Festival with the experience of travelling to Edinburgh.

Instead of communicating only:

> “Take the train to Edinburgh.”

the campaign could communicate:

> **“Your festival experience starts on the journey.”**

This allowed ScotRail to become part of the cultural experience rather than simply a transport provider.

# 25 — Project Takeaway

The ScotRail × Edinburgh International Festival project gave me experience developing a campaign across both **digital and physical customer environments**.

The key idea was to connect the excitement of the destination with the journey itself.

The campaign therefore moved beyond:

**“Travel to Edinburgh.”**

towards:

**“The journey is part of the experience.”**

The work demonstrated how one campaign concept can be adapted across:

**Social → Display → Website → Railway → Bus Station → Festival**

while maintaining a consistent visual and strategic identity.
