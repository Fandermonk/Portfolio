# Sheffield Mind — Marketing & Fundraising Campaign Case Study

## Project Overview
**Client:** Sheffield Mind  
**Project:** Awareness, Participation & Sustainable Funding Campaign  
**Budget:** £500  
**Focus:** Charity awareness, community participation, fundraising and supporter engagement

I worked on a marketing campaign for Sheffield Mind designed to increase awareness of its services, encourage people to participate in the organisation's work, and explore a more sustainable supporter/funding model than relying only on annual subscriptions.

The campaign was developed against a competitive UK charity landscape where organisations compete for attention, participation and donations.

---

## 01 — The Challenge

The campaign needed to solve two connected problems:

- Increase awareness of Sheffield Mind and its services.
- Give people a stronger reason to participate and support the organisation on an ongoing basis.

The proposition was therefore broader than a traditional donation message. Participation could provide access to:

- Training
- Volunteering
- Exclusive events
- Networking
- Publicity and recognition opportunities
- A stronger connection with Sheffield Mind's community

### Core Journey

**Awareness → Participation → Community → Ongoing Support → Sustainable Funding**

---

## 02 — Objectives

- Increase awareness of Sheffield Mind
- Promote its services and mission
- Increase charity participation
- Encourage volunteering and community involvement
- Create opportunities for recurring supporter engagement
- Develop a clearer value proposition around participation
- Generate funding through marketing and outreach

---

## 03 — Target Audience

### Primary Audiences
- Existing Sheffield Mind supporters
- Local community members
- Potential volunteers
- People interested in mental-health advocacy
- Local professionals and businesses
- People interested in community events and networking

### Audience Motivations

**Community-minded people:** belonging and volunteering  
**Professionals:** networking and development  
**Potential volunteers:** training and meaningful involvement  
**Existing supporters:** deeper engagement with Sheffield Mind

---

## 04 — Campaign Strategy

The campaign was structured as a full-funnel journey:

```text
AWARENESS
   ↓
SHEFFIELD MIND SERVICES
   ↓
CAMPAIGN PROPOSITION
   ↓
PARTICIPATION
   ↓
EVENTS / VOLUNTEERING / TRAINING
   ↓
COMMUNITY
   ↓
ONGOING SUPPORT
```

The strategy connected awareness and fundraising rather than treating them as separate activities.
