# Sheffield Mind

## A corporate-support campaign for stronger workplaces and better mental health

**Project type:** Fundraising and awareness campaign concept  
**Focus:** Corporate support scheme, campaign strategy and social-media creative  
**Audience:** Small and large businesses across the Sheffield community

Sheffield Mind provides essential support for people experiencing mental-health challenges. This project explored how a corporate-support scheme could help local businesses contribute regularly, gain meaningful benefits and increase awareness of the charity’s work.

## The challenge

The charity sector is competitive, and smaller organisations can struggle to achieve the visibility and recurring funding available to national charities. Sheffield Mind needed a campaign that made the value of corporate involvement clear: businesses could support vital local services while creating a healthier, more informed workplace culture.

## The campaign objective

The campaign proposed a regular corporate-support model rather than relying only on annual contributions. It aimed to:

- Build awareness of Sheffield Mind and the support it provides
- Encourage local businesses to become long-term partners
- Create a more reliable funding stream for future services
- Offer practical benefits such as training, volunteering, events, networking and visibility opportunities

## Strategy

The core audience was local business decision-makers, so LinkedIn and Facebook were selected as the primary channels. The creative approach combines direct mental-health messaging with an accessible business proposition: better-informed workplaces, stronger local communities and meaningful, visible action.

## Creative direction

The campaign uses confident blue typography, human photography and clear calls to action to make a sensitive subject feel both serious and approachable. The social assets adapt the same message for feed posts, story formats and event promotion, keeping the campaign recognisable across placements.

## Campaign concept

An online webinar provides a practical entry point for businesses. It frames mental-health support as an action organisations can take now, offering expert insights, practical strategies and a clear invitation to engage further with Sheffield Mind.

## Considerations

The concept was designed with a modest proposed budget of **£450**, covering content production, promotion, venue considerations and printed materials. The aim was to keep campaign costs proportionate to the fundraising objective and create reusable assets for future activity.
