# Owlspriority Immigration — Digital Marketing Campaign Case Study

## Project Overview

**Client:** Owlspriority Immigration  
**Project:** Visa Eligibility Check & Canada Immigration Marketing Programme  
**Focus:** Lead Generation, Paid Media, Landing Pages, SEO, Content Marketing & Customer Lifecycle Marketing

I contributed to the digital marketing ecosystem supporting Owlspriority Immigration's monthly **Visa Eligibility Check Programme**.

The work covered multiple stages of the customer journey — from paid acquisition and campaign creative through landing-page experiences, SEO content and ongoing email communication for signed-up customers.

The overall marketing approach combined **Google Ads, Meta Ads, landing pages, SEO, content marketing and email newsletters** to attract prospective immigration customers, communicate relevant Canadian immigration information and encourage users to take an eligibility assessment.

---

## The Challenge

Immigration marketing requires a balance between acquisition and education.

Prospective applicants often need to understand:

- Whether they may be eligible
- Which Canadian immigration pathway could be relevant
- What regulations or requirements have changed
- What new Canadian immigration plans could mean for them
- What their next step should be

The challenge was therefore not simply to generate traffic, but to build a connected journey from **initial awareness to eligibility-check interest and continued customer engagement**.
