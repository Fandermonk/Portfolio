# Owlspriority Immigration

## Building an integrated acquisition and customer-engagement journey for Canadian immigration services

**Project type:** Digital marketing campaign  
**Focus:** Paid media, landing pages, SEO content, campaign design and email marketing

Owlspriority Immigration ran a recurring Visa Eligibility Check programme for people exploring opportunities to study, work or settle in Canada. I contributed across the customer journey: attracting prospective applicants through paid campaigns, helping them understand the offer on landing pages, and maintaining engagement through useful follow-up content.

## The challenge

Immigration decisions are high-consideration. Prospective applicants need clear, trustworthy guidance before they are ready to take an eligibility check. The campaign needed to do more than generate traffic: it had to connect awareness, education, conversion and ongoing communication in one coherent experience.

## My role

I supported the campaign across:

- Google Ads and Meta campaign management
- Paid-media messaging and visual creative
- Conversion-focused landing-page design and copy
- Immigration and SEO website content
- Monthly newsletters and customer communications
- Campaign coordination across acquisition and retention touchpoints

## Campaign strategy

The programme was built around a simple journey:

**Discover → Learn → Check eligibility → Sign up → Stay informed**

Google Search captured users already looking for immigration information, while Meta creative generated interest through direct, visual messaging. The landing page then explained the free eligibility-check value proposition and gave prospects a clear next step. Educational website content and monthly newsletters continued the relationship after sign-up.

## Key deliverables

### Paid media

Created and adapted campaign creative for Google and Meta placements, combining clear eligibility messaging, Canadian imagery and a consistent red, white and black visual system. The work was designed to be trustworthy, accessible and action-oriented.

### Landing page

Supported a conversion-focused landing-page journey with a clear hero, concise benefits, a straightforward form, pathway information, trust-building content and repeated opportunities to take the eligibility check.

### SEO and website content

Managed immigration-focused content that addressed common research-stage questions and created clearer pathways from information to the eligibility programme.

### Customer lifecycle emails

Developed monthly newsletters around visa-regulation changes and Canadian immigration-plan updates. Each translated timely information into approachable customer-facing content and included a relevant next step.

## Outcome

The wider digital acquisition programme generated **3,000+ qualified leads**. More importantly, the connected campaign system gave potential customers a consistent experience from their first ad interaction through to ongoing education and re-engagement.
