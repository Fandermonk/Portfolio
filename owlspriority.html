Visa Eligibility Check & Canada Immigration Marketing Programme

Project overview

I contributed across the Visa Eligibility Check Programme: paid acquisition, campaign creative, landing-page experiences, SEO content and ongoing email communication for prospective Canadian immigration customers.

The brief

A journey that balances acquisition with education.

Prospective applicants needed clarity on eligibility, relevant Canadian pathways, changing regulations and the next step. The programme was designed as one connected system: Discover → Learn → Check Eligibility → Sign Up → Stay Informed.

DiscoverLearnCheck eligibilityStay informed

Paid media

Capture intent on Google.

Google Search activity supported people already exploring visa and immigration options. Campaign management, search-focused messaging, ad-copy coordination and landing-page alignment guided high-intent users into the eligibility assessment.

Google Search → Ad → Landing Page → Visa Eligibility Check → Lead

Paid media

Make the opportunity clear on Meta.

Meta campaigns used direct-response creative, placement-specific variations and clear, trustworthy messaging to build awareness and encourage the next action. The work kept campaign creative aligned with the landing-page promise.

Conversion

Turn campaign attention into an eligibility check.

The landing page made the free eligibility-check offer clear through a focused hero, benefit-led information, a simple form, pathway context, trust-building content and repeated calls to action.

Ad Click → Understand the Offer → Complete Form → Eligibility Assessment

Education

Support the research stage with useful content.

SEO-oriented website content answered immigration questions, developed relevant search topics and made the pathway from research to the eligibility programme easier to understand.

Search → Information → Trust → Eligibility Check

Lifecycle

Keep customers informed after sign-up.

Monthly customer newsletters translated complex immigration developments into accessible updates with relevant next-step calls to action. Each email continued the relationship after the first conversion.

Outcome

One connected marketing ecosystem.

Paid acquisition created awareness and captured intent; the landing page supported conversion; content answered questions; and lifecycle email kept customers engaged with changing immigration information.

3,000+qualified leads generated through the digital acquisition programme.
